The AGI Startup Landscape

Three Lanes, Three Very Different Bets

Every AGI-adjacent startup — and increasingly, every researcher's career choice — falls into one of three strategic lanes.

🔴
Lane 1 — Frontier Scale-First
Training your own foundation model from scratch. Capital-intensive, research-credibility-gated, and the slowest feedback loop of the three.
Explore Lane 1 →
 🔵
Lane 2 — Applied & Agentic Layer
Building on top of existing frontier models. Fast shipping cadence, flat teams, and the most legible path to revenue.
Explore Lane 2 →
 🟢
Lane 3 — Narrow Research Bet
A small, senior team betting everything on one research thesis. No product, no revenue plan — just conviction and capital.
